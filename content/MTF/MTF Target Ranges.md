In the process of writing my own research paper (Still WIP!) I got a few common target ranges together, I will go over them here and share my personal recommendations.

## AuthPATH
[AusPATH](https://auspath.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Standards-of-Care-Digital-NOV-17.pdf), the Australian guidelines recommend maintaining estradiol concerntrations above 250 pmol/L, in accordance with Cheung et al. 

## The Medical Journal of Australia
[Ada S Cheung](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/authored-by/Cheung/Ada+S) in [DOI 10.5694/mja2.50259](https://doi.org/10.5694/mja2.50259) recommend targeting estradiol levels of 250–600 pmol/L and total testosterone levels < 2 nmol/L

## WPATH Standards of Care Version 8

The [SOC8](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/pdf/10.1080/26895269.2022.2100644) state that serum testosterone levels should be less than 50 ng/dL and serum estradiol should be in the range of 100-200 pg/mL.

## Journal of Clinical Endocrinology & Metabolism

Serum estradiol should not exceed the peak physiologic range: 100–200 pg/mL.

The target range I settled on for myself was for my E2 trough levels to sit around 650pmol/L. now I am specifically using Estradiol Undecylate so my peaks aren't much higher than my lowest points (Estimated range of 680pmol/L peaks and 663pmol/L lows)[^1]

I still feel a minimum of 650pmol/L is a useful value for most individuals, the usual dosages for EV(5 day cycle), EEn(7 day cycle), EC(7 day cycle) and EUn(28/30 day cycle) all keep you above that number anyway.
