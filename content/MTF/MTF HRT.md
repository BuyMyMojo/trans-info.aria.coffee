HRT stands for hormone replacement therapy.

Everyone already has hormones in their body, AMAB individuals typically have more Testosterone and AFAB individuals typically have more oestrogen.

HRT can be used to change the dominant hormone in your body giving you many effects (See [[MTF Effects]]) affirming for your gender.
