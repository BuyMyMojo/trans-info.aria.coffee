# Voice Training

Modifying your voice to align with your gender identity is a powerful tool. It involves changing habits of pitch, resonance, and weight.

## The Basics

* **Pitch**: How high or low your voice is (frequency).
* **Resonance**: The "timbre" or brightness of your voice. (Chest voice vs. Head voice).
  * *Feminine*: Brighter, smaller space (Head/Throat resonance).
  * *Masculine*: Darker, larger space (Chest resonance).
* **Vocal Weight**: How "heavy" or "buzzy" the voice sounds.

## For Transmasculine Folks

Testosterone *will* thicken vocal folds and drop your pitch, but "training" can help you settle into that new range comfortably and learn "masculine" speech patterns (intonation).
