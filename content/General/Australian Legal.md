# Australian Legal Resources

Navigating the legal side of transition in Australia can be complex as laws vary by state.

> [!warning]
> Laws change frequently. Always double-check with official government sources for your specific state.

## National (Federal)

### Medicare & Centrelink

You can change your gender marker with Medicare/Centrelink:

* [Services Australia - Centerlink Gender Update Guide](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/updating-your-gender-details-with-us?context=64107#a1)
* [Services Australia - Medicare Gender Update Guide](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/how-you-update-your-name-date-birth-or-gender-for-medicare?context=60092#updatingyourgender)

### Passports

Australia allows 'M', 'F', or 'X' markers.

* [Australian Passport Office - Name and Gender](https://www.passports.gov.au/change-name-or-gender)
